# References

Drop reference images / clips for future art direction here.

For v0.1 (hero scene) the direction was taken from two screen-recorded clips shared in the
first build session (not committed — they are third-party social videos, used for mood only):

1. **Framer/AI website showcase** — dark editorial hero, an orbital ribbon of light around a
   central subject, large serif headline with italic accents ("Built for the curious"),
   glassmorphic pill navigation.
2. **"Make your website stop the scroll" 3D showcase** — pastel dusk sea with a mirror-calm
   reflective surface and floating glass bubbles; deep-blue underwater scene with luminous
   jellyfish, coral and volumetric light rays; serif headline with an italic word.

What was taken: *mood, palette families, composition ideas* (mirror horizon, floating glass,
bioluminescent life, god rays, serif + italic typography, glass nav).
What was **not** taken: any layout, copy, branding, model, texture or asset. Everything in the
scene is procedural geometry and shaders written for AETHER.
